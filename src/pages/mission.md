Time is one of the fundamental dimensions through which people coordinate activities with others and organize their daily lives. However, the information needed for this is often fragmented and isolated across many different digital solutions. To address this challenge, the Momentful I/O initiative is developing open infrastructure for temporal content.

## Our vision

We want a web where temporal content can reach people wherever it is useful. People should be able to choose how they discover and use it, while publishers retain control over what they share.

## Our mission

We develop free and open-source tools for publishing, discovering and navigating temporal content. We connect existing systems and provide reusable components that developers can build on for their own applications.

## Our philosophy

Publish once &ndash; reuse everywhere. Temporal content should remain under the publisher's control while being reusable by many applications through open interfaces and federation. Temporal content should not live inside one application. The infrastructure should be generic. Applications become replaceable.

## Our principles

- Free software
- Decentralization and federation
- Open standards and documented APIs
- Modular, reusable components
- Self-hosting and local use
- Privacy and independent offline access
- Accessible interfaces and clear documentation

## Contributing

Momentful I/O is an evolving initiative by Yoveno GmbH. We welcome people who want to build applications, connect existing systems, improve documentation or bring experience from their own communities. Every useful integration helps make temporal content more accessible and reusable.
