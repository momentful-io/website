Calendify is the publishing platform within Momentful I/O. It gives organizations, communities and individuals a place to publish and maintain temporal content, making it available for people to follow and for other applications to use. The idea is simple: publish once, reuse everywhere.

Calendify brings publishing, hosting and subscriptions together. Publishers can organize and manage their content, while readers can subscribe to the calendars that matter to them. Events are one possible content type, alongside other forms of content organized around time. As part of Momentful I/O’s open infrastructure, Calendify is being developed to support independent publishers and make their content reusable across websites and applications.

You can explore Calendify today at [calendify.com](https://calendify.com), which serves as a showroom and reference implementation. This existing implementation is closed source: its source code is not currently publicly available and serves only as a case study. A new open-source implementation is in development and will be available soon for public use.
