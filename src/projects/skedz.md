Skedz turns published temporal content into personal, interactive agendas. Within Momentful I/O, it helps people navigate complex temporal structures and choose how to spend their time. Its initial focus is conferences, with a long-term vision that extends to other structured sequences of activities, including travel, training and educational plans.

With Skedz, people can browse and filter schedules, select sessions and build their own agenda. It works with common calendar and conference schedule formats, allowing content from different publishers to be used in one familiar interface. Schedules and personal choices are stored on the user’s device, so previously loaded content remains available offline.

Skedz is already available as a Progressive Web App for mobile and desktop browsers. You can explore it at [skedz.org](https://skedz.org). The project is open source, with its source code available on [GitHub](https://github.com/ysorge/skedz). Development currently focuses on conference schedules; broader uses are part of its future direction.
